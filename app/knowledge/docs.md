# MediCare Health Partner

## About MediCare

MediCare is an online health partner that combines clinic appointment booking with an online pharmacy. You can browse six specialist clinics, reserve a visit, check medicine prices, and place a demo order for delivery. The assistant on the website answers questions about the service and general health topics. MediCare is not an emergency service and does not replace a doctor.

## Website sections

- Home: an overview of the service and a shortcut to book an appointment.
- Clinics: six departments, each with a short description and a booking button.
- Pharmacy: the medicine catalogue with names, strengths, prices, and an order button.
- Contact: phone number, email address, location, and opening hours.
- Assistant: a chat window that can navigate the site, book appointments, open the pharmacy, and answer questions.

### How to book an appointment

1. Open the Clinics section or ask the assistant to book a clinic.
2. Choose the clinic: General Medicine, Dental Care, Pediatrics, Cardiology, Dermatology, or Orthopedics.
3. Enter your name, preferred date, and preferred time.
4. Confirm the booking. The confirmation appears on screen.

Appointments can be cancelled or moved up to two hours before the scheduled time by calling the clinic. Clinics open every day from 9:00 to 21:00. Video consultations are not offered yet. Insurance is not processed online; patients pay at the clinic and claim separately.

### How to order

1. Open the Pharmacy section or ask the assistant to buy a medicine.
2. Choose the medicine and the quantity.
3. Enter the delivery name and address.
4. Confirm the demo order. No real payment is taken on this website.

Delivery in Cairo and Giza is same day for orders placed before 18:00. Delivery costs 50 EGP and is free for orders over 500 EGP. Other areas are delivered within two to three working days. The demo checkout shows the item total only and does not add delivery fees. Real orders are paid cash on delivery; the demo checkout collects no payment details. Prescription-only medicines require a valid prescription that is checked by the pharmacist before dispatch. Unopened medicines can be returned within seven days with the receipt. Cold-chain items cannot be returned.

## Safety and privacy

MediCare gives general health information only. It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe medicines, or replace a qualified clinician. Prescription medicines are dispensed only after a pharmacist checks a valid prescription. Do not share sensitive medical details in the chat. The demo stores no payment card data. If you have an emergency, call local emergency services immediately.
